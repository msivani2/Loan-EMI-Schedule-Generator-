Two things we handle for you

Floating-rate resets

When the rate changes in month N, the EMI is recalculated on the outstanding balance over the remaining months, so every later instalment updates.

Rounding residue at closure

Monthly interest is rounded to the paisa, which leaves a small leftover. The last instalment absorbs it, so the closing balance is exactly 0.00.
